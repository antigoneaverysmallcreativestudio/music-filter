MUSIC FILTER — TEST BUILD
Designed by Antigone, a very small creative studio.

This build is for testing on another Mac. It has a development signature and
has not been notarized by Apple: macOS may block it from opening. It is not
the public distribution package yet.

INSTALLATION
1. Open the disk image and drag Music Filter into Applications.
2. Eject the disk image, then open the app from Applications.
3. Its icon appears in the menu bar. Open Music and play a song.
4. Allow the app to control Music when macOS asks.

If you previously denied permission, enable it in System Settings > Privacy &
Security > Automation, then quit and reopen Music Filter. Changing the permission
alone does not yet restart the filter.

USAGE
Exclude the current song, album or artist from the menu bar icon. Artist rules
include collaborations identified in the metadata. Manage exclusions opens
the list and brings its window forward. Right-click an entry to remove its rule.

Automatic filtering skips excluded songs when they begin.
Filter upcoming songs remains experimental and is off by default. It requires
Accessibility permission. Music must use French or English, with an open,
non-minimized window and its playback queue displayed. It may be behind other
apps. Matching upcoming songs can then be removed from this queue without
editing the saved playlist. This does not guarantee silence during a late transition.

Start with Music is enabled by default. macOS may ask you to allow background
launching. Quit really stops the app; you can always reopen it from Applications.

LANGUAGE AND COMPATIBILITY
One application contains both languages; there is no language choice to make
during installation. French is used only when your primary system language is
French. Every other primary language, including Arabic, uses English, even if
French is a secondary preference. The name stays Music Filter in all languages.
Appearance follows the system. Your language preferences are not changed.
Universal Intel and Apple Silicon build, targeting macOS 14 or later.
Live checks so far: macOS 27.2.1 on Apple Silicon. Other versions and Intel
still need live validation.

DATA AND UPDATES
No developer exclusions or preferences are included in this package.
Your exclusions are stored locally at:
~/Library/Application Support/Music Filter/exclusions.json
Artwork display may send artist and album names to Apple's public services.
No Apple account credentials are sent.
To update, quit Music Filter and replace the app in Applications. Keep your
local data. There is no built-in automatic updater yet.
