# Third-party resources

The MIT license in this repository applies to the project's source code.

Apple's SF Symbols are used through macOS APIs. Symbol-derived icon artwork
needed by the app is kept in `Resources`; editable sources and the app-icon PDF
input are kept separately in `Artwork`. These Apple resources remain subject to
Apple's applicable terms; the project's MIT license does not relicense them. Review
[Apple's SF Symbols information](https://developer.apple.com/sf-symbols/) before
reusing or redistributing these assets outside this application.

Album covers and artist portraits are obtained at runtime from Apple's public
catalog and artist pages. No catalog artwork, listening history, exclusion list,
or artwork cache is included in the source distribution. Rights to those images
remain with their respective owners.
